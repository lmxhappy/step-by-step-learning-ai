Scaling New Frontiers: Insights into Large Recommendation Models

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.00714

Company: Huawei

Overview: Large recommendation models, represented by Meta's generative recommendation model HSTU, have broken through the bottleneck of model scaling. This paper tries to understand these scaling laws in depth: it analyzes the scaling laws of large recommendation models and explores where they come from. It offers insights into the future development of large recommendation models.

Credibility: offline

Recommendation: worth reading
