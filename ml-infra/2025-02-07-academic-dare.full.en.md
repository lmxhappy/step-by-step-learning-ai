DARE: Distillation-Enhanced Ranking for Efficient Recommendation

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.18779

Company: Xiangnan He.

Area: distillation

Overview: This paper proposes a distillation-enhanced ranking method that distills the knowledge of a large model into a small model to achieve efficient ranking. Specifically, DARE first distills the large model's knowledge into the small model, then uses the small model for ranking. DARE also introduces a new distillation module that combines the large model's knowledge with the small model's ranking to achieve efficient ranking.
