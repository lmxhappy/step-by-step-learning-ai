GraphHash: Graph Clustering Enables Parameter Efficiency in Recommender Systems

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.17245

Area: Feature-value hashing

Company: Snap

Overview: Proposes a graph-based method that implements a way of hashing feature values, thereby compressing and optimizing the embedding table. Using graph-based collaborative signals, it applies modularity-based bipartite graph clustering on the user-item interaction graph to reduce the embedding table size.
