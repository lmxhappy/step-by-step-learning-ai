SyNeg: LLM-Driven Synthetic Hard-Negatives for Dense Retrieval

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.17250

Company: Huawei

Area: Retrieval + negative sample generation + LLM

Overview: This work proposes a framework that uses LLMs to synthesize high-quality hard negatives. It designs a multi-attribute self-reflection prompting strategy to guide the LLM in generating hard negatives.
