Semantic Retrieval at Walmart

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.04637

Company: Walmart

Area: embedding-based retrieval

Overview: Proposes a hybrid retrieval system that combines a traditional inverted index with neural retrieval. It introduces a new method for selecting negative samples in training, improving the handling of complex tail queries. It also shares lessons learned from successfully deploying the system in an e-commerce setting.

Credibility: production

Recommendation: worth reading
