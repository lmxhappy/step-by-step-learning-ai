Diff4Steer: Steerable Diffusion Prior for Generative Music Retrieval with Semantic Guidance

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.04746

Company: Google

Area: retrieval + diffusion

Overview: Applies diffusion-based generative methods to music retrieval. The generated embeddings are of high quality and reflect the latent distribution of the data, and they can incorporate the uncertainty in user preference modeling, improving retrieval: it ensures diversity while delivering competitive retrieval and ranking performance.

Credibility: offline
