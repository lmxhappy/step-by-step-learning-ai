1 PRECISE: Pre-training Sequential Recommenders with Collaborative and Semantic Information

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.06308

Company: Tencent

Area: LLM + multi-scenario

Overview: (1) Get semantic embeddings of items from a large language model and fuse them with the recommendation model's item embeddings to strengthen the model. (2) Apply a transformer network trained across all scenarios to a specific scenario, so the model perceives global interests and interest estimates become more accurate. Using LLMs to enhance behavior sequences is a good direction and can improve sequence modeling.

Credibility: production

Recommendation: worth a look
