Graph-Sequential Alignment and Uniformity: Toward Enhanced Recommendation Systems

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.04276

Company: Salesforce

Area: behavior sequence + GNN

Overview: Previously the two were independent. This paper wants to combine them: train them simultaneously, share a unified embedding space, and require alignment and uniformity between the two, designing a corresponding loss for each. Arguably a good direction for broadening behavior sequence modeling.

Credibility: offline

Recommendation: worth reading
