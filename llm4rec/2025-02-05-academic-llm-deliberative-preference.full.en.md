Large Language Models for Recommendation with Deliberative User Preference Alignment

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.02061

Company: Xiangnan He. There are Meta authors on it, but not as first or corresponding author. By comparison, Xiangnan He is the bigger name.

Area: LLM recommendation + reasoning

Overview: It simply borrows the recent reasoning capability of large models for recommender systems. The LLM reasons over the user's historical behavior to infer the user's preferences, and then makes the prediction.

It coins a term for applying reasoning to recommender systems: the Deliberative Recommendation task. This is worth learning from: be bold, dare to put forward ideas, such as a new branch of research; it might just work out. That is more interesting than always following. I also can't help remarking how fast their team is at applying new concepts. Most importantly, though, I think applying this idea makes sense.

Credibility: offline

Recommendation: worth reading
