# Recommendation algorithm paper

> [中文版](2024-12-13-sprec.md)

1 SPRec: Leveraging Self-Play to Debias Preference Alignment for Large Language Model-based Recommendations

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.09243

Company: He Xiangnan's team, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)

Area: LLM recommendation

Overview:

(1) Motivation: DPO (Direct Preference Optimization), proposed in 2023, combines chosen and rejected response pairs, letting the LLM learn the user's ranking preferences and develop a more nuanced understanding of fine-grained personalized information. But because of its internal mechanism, it inevitably introduces significant bias. This bias leads to severe homogenization: the LLM produces items with similar names or content.

(2) The paper's method: each tuning iteration first does supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on the positives from offline data, then direct preference optimization (DPO), treating the SFT data as positives and the previous iteration's predicted outputs as negatives (DPO's input is a triple of (input, preferred output, less preferred output)). The model "self-plays" by reweighting the DPO loss based on its predictions. The higher an item ranks in the predictions, the larger the penalty it gets, while the SFT step strengthens the ranking of positives. This self-play learning process adaptively suppresses all undesired items (bias) over time while staying aligned with the positives. Extensive experiments on public datasets show that SPRec effectively improves accuracy and fairness metrics, showing its potential as a practical and efficient solution for large-scale recommender systems.

Credibility: offline

Recommendation: worth a look
