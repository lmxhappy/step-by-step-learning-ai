1 Describe What You See with Multimodal Large Language Models to Enhance Video Recommendations

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09789

Company: Spotify

Area: large models

Overview:

A large model that analyzes video extracts high-level semantic descriptions of the video, which helps improve recommendation.

The model is Alibaba's Qwen-VL, used without any tuning. The input is the video's key frames.

Takeaways:

(1) Large models can extract valuable information about items that could not be obtained before, which helps improve performance.

Credibility: offline

Recommendation: worth a look
