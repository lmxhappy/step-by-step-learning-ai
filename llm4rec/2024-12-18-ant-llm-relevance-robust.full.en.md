Boosting LLM-based Relevance Modeling with Distribution-Aware Robust Learning

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.12504

Company: Alipay Search

Area: search + LLM

Overview: The background is that LLMs used to assess search relevance struggle to distinguish fine-grained relevance (e.g., strongly relevant, weakly relevant, irrelevant). The method extracts out-of-distribution (OOD) data from a large amount of unlabeled data and uses it to strengthen the LLM.

Credibility: production

Recommendation: worth reading
