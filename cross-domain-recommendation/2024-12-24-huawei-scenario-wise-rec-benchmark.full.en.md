Scenario-Wise Rec: A Multi-Scenario Recommendation Benchmark

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.17374

Company: Huawei

Area: Multi-scenario recommendation

Overview: For the multi-scenario recommendation (MSR) task, the paper proposes a benchmark framework, Scenario-Wise Rec, which includes 6 public datasets and 12 baseline models, and provides a training and evaluation pipeline. With these multi-scenario datasets, models can be compared fairly, especially against state-of-the-art (SOTA) models. Work like this makes a big contribution to the development of MSR. Kudos.

Credibility: Production
