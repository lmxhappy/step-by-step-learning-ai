Counterfactual Learning-Driven Representation Disentanglement for Search-Enhanced Recommendation

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.18631

Alibaba

Area: search-enhanced recommendation

Overview: In short, item features from the search scenario are split, via counterfactual learning, into two kinds: query-independent and query-related. Only the former is used in the recommendation model, which improves the model, because the latter has a negative effect on the recommendation model.

Credibility: production

Recommendation: worth a look
