1 Branches, Assemble! Multi-Branch Cooperation Network for Large-Scale Click-Through Rate Prediction at Taobao

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.13057

Company: Alibaba Taobao

Area: feature crossing

Overview: "Branch" here means a different way of modeling feature crossing, e.g., implicit dense modeling or linear cross modeling; each modeling method is one branch. The paper proposes a multi-branch cooperation mechanism, i.e., the branches cooperate with each other. Just like modeling with multiple groups of experts, feature-crossing modeling is also trying out modular designs with parallel modules that cooperate and divide the work among themselves.

Credibility: production. 0.09point increase in CTR, 1.49% growth in deals, and 1.62% rise in GMV.

Recommendation: worth a look
