USM: Unbiased Survey Modeling for Limiting Negative User Experiences in Recommendation Systems

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.10674

Company: TikTok

Overview: Use user surveys to adjust recommendation results. That is, distribute surveys to users in the feed, model the user feedback collected from the surveys, and integrate the model's predictions into the recommender system. Looks like it belongs to a small TikTok business.
