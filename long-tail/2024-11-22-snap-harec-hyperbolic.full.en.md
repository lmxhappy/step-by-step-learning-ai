HARec: Hyperbolic Graph-LLM Alignment for Exploration and Exploitation in Recommender Systems

Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.13865

Company: Snap

Area: long tail + LLM

Overview: Hyperbolic space is curved, non-Euclidean geometry. Its key property is that distance grows exponentially as you move away from the origin, analogous to a tree where the number of nodes grows exponentially with depth. It suits modeling hierarchical relations: a user may be interested in a broad category of items (e.g. "electronics") and also in a more specific subcategory (e.g. "phones"), and hyperbolic geometry can model this layered interest structure more clearly through embeddings.

Credibility: production

Recommendation: worth a look
